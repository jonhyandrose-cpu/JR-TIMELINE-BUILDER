    DIGITAL PHOTOGRAPHY & VIDEOGRAPHY SERVICE AGREEMENT

    TERMS & CONDITIONS — JONHY & ROSE PHOTOGRAPHY STUDIO

    For purposes of this Agreement, “Jonhy & Rose” refers to Jonhy & Rose Photography Studio and, where applicable, the photographers, videographers, filmmakers, assistants, editors, and team members assigned by the Studio to provide the contracted services.

    The “Client” refers to the person or persons entering into this Agreement and contracting the services described in the corresponding proposal, invoice, or booking confirmation.

    The Client is entitled to the photography and/or videography services specifically included in the selected package, proposal, invoice, or written booking confirmation.

    I. RESERVATION & PAYMENT

    I.a) Standard Payment Plan

    Unless the Client has selected a Preferred Full Payment Rate or another payment arrangement specifically stated in writing, the standard reservation process requires:

    • A signed contract; and
    • A $300 USD non-refundable retainer.

    The event date is not considered officially reserved until both the signed Agreement and required retainer have been received by Jonhy & Rose.

    The $300 USD retainer is applied toward the total contracted amount.

    The remaining balance must be paid in full no later than 30 calendar days before the event date.

    Failure to complete the remaining payment by the due date may result in suspension or cancellation of the contracted services. The initial retainer remains non-refundable.

    I.b) Preferred Full Payment Rate

    From time to time, Jonhy & Rose may offer a Preferred Full Payment Rate, which provides a reduced or specially negotiated total price in exchange for receiving full payment in advance.

    When the Client selects this option, 100% of the agreed Preferred Full Payment Rate is due at booking, together with the signed Agreement.

    The event date is not officially reserved until the signed Agreement and full payment have been received.

    Because this rate is granted specifically in consideration of advance full payment, it cannot be combined with another promotion, booking bonus, discount, or payment plan unless Jonhy & Rose expressly agrees otherwise in writing.

    The Preferred Full Payment Rate cannot be retroactively converted to the Standard Payment Plan after payment has been completed.

    Cancellation & Refund Policy for Preferred Full Payment Rate

    Because Jonhy & Rose reserves the event date, allocates team availability, begins administrative and planning work, and may decline other inquiries for the same date, the following cancellation policy applies specifically to Preferred Full Payment Rate bookings:

    • Cancellation within 30 calendar days of payment: 80% of the total amount received will be refunded.
    • Cancellation more than 30 calendar days after payment and more than 6 months before the scheduled event date: 50% of the total amount received will be refunded.
    • Cancellation 6 months or less before the scheduled event date: all payments are non-refundable.

    Any refund will be calculated based on the amount actually received by Jonhy & Rose.

    Payment processing fees, banking fees, transfer fees, currency conversion charges, or other non-recoverable transaction costs are non-refundable.

    All cancellation requests must be submitted in writing via email. The date Jonhy & Rose receives the written cancellation request will determine the applicable refund tier.

    Any refund due under this section will be processed within 30 calendar days after written cancellation is received.

    I.c) Rescheduling

    Requests to change the event date must be submitted in writing and are subject to Jonhy & Rose’s availability.

    If Jonhy & Rose is available for the requested new date and accepts the change in writing, payments already received may be transferred to the new event date.

    A change of date is not automatically guaranteed.

    If Jonhy & Rose is unavailable for the requested new date, the request will be treated as a cancellation and the cancellation terms corresponding to the Client’s selected payment method will apply based on the date the rescheduling request was received.

    Any changes in location, scope of coverage, travel requirements, team requirements, or services resulting from the new date may require an adjustment to the original price.

    I.d) Venue, Resort & External Vendor Requirements

    Both parties acknowledge that Jonhy & Rose is an independent contractor and is not an employee or representative of the resort, hotel, venue, or wedding planning company.

    The Client is responsible for any external vendor fees, vendor passes, day passes, permits, access fees, or hotel accommodations required by the venue for Jonhy & Rose and its assigned team.

    Vendor meals are required on the wedding day when contracted coverage exceeds 5 hours.

    Any venue-specific requirement that creates an additional expense must be covered by the Client unless otherwise agreed in writing.

    I.e) Third-Party Services

    Jonhy & Rose is an independent company and is not responsible for errors, delays, omissions, or service failures caused by resorts, venues, coordinators, planners, makeup artists, hairstylists, florists, DJs, transportation providers, or other third-party vendors.

    II. PRE-EVENT CONFIRMATION & WEDDING TIMELINE

    Prior to the event, the Client agrees to provide Jonhy & Rose with the information reasonably necessary to plan the photography and/or videography coverage.

    Jonhy & Rose may provide the Client with a questionnaire requesting information regarding the wedding day, locations, schedule, family photographs, wedding party, priorities, special moments, and other relevant details.

    Using this information, Jonhy & Rose may prepare a recommended photography and/or videography timeline based on:

    • Contracted coverage hours
    • Client priorities
    • Ceremony and reception schedule
    • Venue logistics
    • Travel time between locations
    • Available natural light
    • Sunset time
    • Photography and videography requirements

    The timeline represents Jonhy & Rose’s professional recommendation and is intended as a planning guide. Wedding-day circumstances may require adjustments in real time.

    The Client is responsible for communicating important requests, specific family combinations, special traditions, surprises, or other priority moments before the event.

    Requests and final information may be submitted to jonhyandrose@gmail.com.

    III. COOPERATION

    III.a) Both parties agree to maintain reasonable communication and cooperation to achieve the best possible photography and/or videography outcome.

    III.b) For family photographs or large group photographs, Jonhy & Rose recommends that the Client designate a family member, wedding party member, planner, or other person familiar with the guests to assist in identifying and gathering the individuals required.

    III.c) Jonhy & Rose cannot guarantee photographs or video of individuals who are unavailable, late, unwilling to participate, or cannot be located during the allocated photography or videography time.

    III.d) Jonhy & Rose is not responsible for missing moments, photographs, people, traditions, surprises, or events that were not communicated beforehand or that occur simultaneously in different locations without sufficient team coverage.

    IV. DELAYS & TIMELINE CHANGES

    The contracted coverage consists of the number of hours specified in the selected package, proposal, invoice, or written booking confirmation.

    Delays caused by makeup, hair, transportation, venue operations, ceremony delays, weather, guests, planners, or other circumstances may reduce the amount of time available for planned photographs or video coverage.

    If the Client or another vendor causes the timeline to run late, Jonhy & Rose cannot guarantee that every originally planned photograph, location, or moment can still be captured within the contracted coverage period.

    Additional coverage may be requested subject to team availability and the applicable additional-time rate.

    V. PHOTOGRAPHY SERVICE & CREATIVE PROCESS

    All final digital images will be edited according to Jonhy & Rose’s signature editorial style.

    Standard editing may include adjustments to:

    • Exposure
    • Contrast
    • White balance
    • Color
    • Tone
    • Cropping
    • General image consistency

    The Client acknowledges that prior to entering into this Agreement, they had the opportunity to review examples of Jonhy & Rose’s work and understands and accepts the Studio’s photographic style and creative approach.

    V.a) Retouching

    Standard editing may include reasonable corrections, when feasible, such as:

    • Removal of distracting people in backgrounds
    • Removal of minor distracting environmental elements
    • Visible signs
    • Minor stains on the couple’s attire
    • Minor adjustments to the bride’s or groom’s hair

    These corrections are performed at the editor’s discretion and are not guaranteed for every photograph.

    More extensive retouching—including scars, extensive skin retouching, closed eyes, body modifications, stains on guests’ clothing, removal of photographers, videographers, coordinators, wedding planners, extensive seaweed removal, reconstruction of backgrounds, or other complex alterations—will be evaluated individually.

    Additional editing fees may apply depending on feasibility and complexity.

    Jonhy & Rose does not create photographic montages or artificially reconstruct moments that did not occur.

    V.b) RAW Photography Files

    Jonhy & Rose does not deliver RAW, unedited, rejected, duplicate, test, or unfinished photography files.

    Selection of the final images is part of Jonhy & Rose’s creative process and professional service.

    VI. VIDEOGRAPHY SERVICE & CREATIVE PROCESS

    The Client acknowledges that videography provided by Jonhy & Rose is a creative and documentary interpretation of the event rather than a continuous recording of every moment unless a specific continuous recording is expressly included in the selected package.

    VI.a) Editing Style

    Final video content is edited according to Jonhy & Rose’s cinematic style and may include:

    • Color grading
    • Sound design
    • Licensed music
    • Natural audio
    • Vows
    • Speeches
    • Narrative editing
    • Cinematic sequencing

    The Client acknowledges and accepts this creative style.

    VI.b) Creative Control

    Jonhy & Rose retains creative discretion regarding filming, camera placement, lens selection, shot selection, editing, storytelling, music, pacing, audio selection, and final presentation.

    Final results may vary depending on available light, weather, venue restrictions, event timeline, audio conditions, guest behavior, and events occurring in real time.

    VI.c) Video Deliverables

    Video deliverables depend exclusively on the selected package and may include:

    • Highlight Film
    • Documentary or extended wedding film
    • Full ceremony video
    • Speeches
    • Social media films or Reels
    • Other specifically listed deliverables

    Only deliverables expressly included in the Client’s selected package, invoice, or written booking confirmation are guaranteed.

    VI.d) RAW Video Footage

    Jonhy & Rose does not deliver RAW or unedited video footage unless a separate written agreement expressly states otherwise.

    VI.e) Audio

    Jonhy & Rose may use professional audio recording equipment when appropriate; however, audio quality may depend on equipment and services provided by third parties.

    Jonhy & Rose is not responsible for audio limitations or failures caused by DJs, sound systems, venue equipment, defective microphones, interference, excessive environmental noise, wind, guests, or other circumstances outside the Studio’s reasonable control.

    VI.f) Music

    Music used in wedding films is selected according to Jonhy & Rose’s creative approach and available licensing options.

    Certain social media platforms or online services may restrict, mute, block, or otherwise limit music playback because of their own copyright or licensing policies. Such platform restrictions are outside Jonhy & Rose’s control.

    VII. COPYRIGHT & CLIENT USAGE LICENSE

    All photographs and videos created by Jonhy & Rose remain protected by applicable copyright law.

    Unless otherwise expressly agreed in writing, copyright ownership is retained by Jonhy & Rose Photography Studio.

    Upon full payment, the Client receives a non-exclusive license to download, reproduce, print, display, and share the delivered photographs and videos for personal, non-commercial use.

    The Client may share the delivered work on personal social media accounts and with family and friends.

    The Client may not sell, commercially license, materially alter, claim authorship of, or use the delivered work for commercial advertising without prior written authorization from Jonhy & Rose.

    VIII. PORTFOLIO, ADVERTISING & PROMOTIONAL USE

    Unless otherwise agreed in writing before the event, the Client grants Jonhy & Rose permission to use photographs and video created during the contracted event for portfolio, website, social media, editorial, publication, competition, advertising, educational, and promotional purposes.

    This may include content published by Jonhy & Rose Photography Studio and its associated brands.

    IX. EXCLUSIVE PROFESSIONAL TEAM

    IX.a) Unless otherwise agreed in writing, Jonhy & Rose will serve as the Client’s professional photography and/or videography team for the services contracted through this Agreement.

    IX.b) Guests may take photographs and videos provided they do not interfere with the professional team, block cameras, occupy designated shooting positions, or materially disrupt the coverage.

    IX.c) Jonhy & Rose is not responsible for missed or compromised photographs, video, or audio caused by interference, obstruction, flashes, mobile devices, guests, content creators, or other vendors.

    If another professional content creator, photographer, or videographer will be present, the Client agrees to inform Jonhy & Rose before the event so reasonable coordination can take place.

    X. DRONE SERVICE

    Drone coverage included in a photography and videography package is primarily intended for videography.

    Drone footage may be used for aerial establishing shots, venue footage, landscapes, wedding setups, and other cinematic elements incorporated into the final wedding films.

    When conditions, timing, and applicable restrictions allow, the photography team may also use the drone to capture a limited number of aerial photographs, including:

    • Venue photographs
    • Wedding or reception setup photographs
    • Surrounding landscapes
    • Group photographs

    Aerial photography is not guaranteed and will be determined by the team according to creative needs, available time, venue conditions, and operational feasibility.

    Drone operation is always subject to:

    • Weather conditions
    • Wind conditions
    • Local aviation regulations
    • Airspace restrictions
    • Venue and resort policies
    • Safety considerations
    • Required property authorization

    Jonhy & Rose does not guarantee drone operation at a specific venue, location, or moment during the event.

    When authorization from a hotel, resort, or venue is required, obtaining that authorization is the Client’s responsibility unless otherwise agreed in writing.

    The drone will not be operated when Jonhy & Rose determines that doing so would be unsafe, prohibited, unauthorized, or inappropriate under the circumstances.

    The inability to operate a drone because of weather, safety concerns, legal restrictions, venue policies, lack of authorization, or other circumstances outside Jonhy & Rose’s reasonable control does not constitute failure to provide the contracted photography or videography service and does not entitle the Client to a refund, discount, credit, or replacement service.

    XI. WEATHER & OUTDOOR EVENTS

    The safety of the Client, guests, team, and equipment takes priority during adverse weather.

    Jonhy & Rose may temporarily suspend outdoor photography, videography, or drone operation when rain, wind, lightning, storms, or other environmental conditions create an unreasonable risk to people or equipment.

    If the Client elects to continue an outdoor event despite adverse conditions, Jonhy & Rose cannot guarantee uninterrupted coverage or the same photographic/video results that would have been possible under normal conditions.

    Weather-related limitations outside Jonhy & Rose’s reasonable control do not automatically entitle the Client to a refund or reduction in the contracted price.

    XII. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY & FORCE MAJEURE

    If Jonhy & Rose is unable to perform the contracted services due to serious illness, injury, emergency, or another circumstance beyond reasonable control, reasonable efforts will be made to provide a qualified replacement when possible.

    If Jonhy & Rose cannot provide the contracted services and no reasonable replacement can be secured, liability will be limited to the amount paid by the Client for the services that cannot be provided.

    In the event of force majeure—including natural disasters, hurricanes, government restrictions, civil emergencies, venue closures, or other circumstances beyond the reasonable control of either party—the parties will make reasonable efforts to reschedule, modify, or otherwise coordinate the contracted services.

    Any refund, credit, rescheduling, or termination resulting from such circumstances will be determined according to this Agreement and the services that can reasonably be performed.

    XIII. FILE BACKUP, LOSS & DATA

    Jonhy & Rose follows a professional file-management and backup workflow; however, no digital storage system can eliminate every possible risk.

    In the unlikely event that photographs or video files are permanently lost, corrupted, destroyed, or stolen before delivery despite reasonable professional precautions, Jonhy & Rose’s maximum liability will be limited to the amount paid for the affected photography and/or videography service.

    Partial loss of material will be evaluated proportionally according to the affected portion of the contracted service.

    XIV. CANCELLATION

    All cancellation requests must be made in writing via email.

    Standard Payment Plan

    For Clients using the Standard Payment Plan, the initial $300 USD retainer is non-refundable.

    Any additional amounts paid will be handled according to the terms of this Agreement and the timing and circumstances of the cancellation.

    Preferred Full Payment Rate

    For Clients who selected the Preferred Full Payment Rate, the specific refund schedule established in Section I.b applies.

    A Client’s failure to respond to repeated reasonable attempts by Jonhy & Rose to obtain information necessary to perform the contracted services does not automatically create a right to a refund.

    XV. COMPLETION & DELIVERY CALENDAR

    Final photographs and video deliverables will generally be delivered within 9 weeks following the event.

    The delivery period begins after completion of the contracted event coverage and may be reasonably affected by workload, production requirements, holidays in Mexico, unforeseen technical circumstances, or other circumstances outside the Studio’s reasonable control.

    Any sneak peeks, previews, Reels, or expedited content are governed by the specific deliverables stated in the selected package.

    XVI. FINAL DELIVERY & FILE RETENTION

    The Client acknowledges that the delivered gallery and final video files represent Jonhy & Rose’s completed creative work.

    RAW photography files and RAW/unedited video footage are not included.

    Final delivered galleries and videos should be downloaded and backed up by the Client.

    Unless another retention period is expressly stated in writing, Jonhy & Rose does not guarantee indefinite archival storage of RAW files, project files, or delivered materials after final delivery.

    XVII. COVERAGE, TRAVEL & ADDITIONAL TIME

    XVII.a) The contracted price includes the photographers, videographers, filmmakers, and/or assistants specifically required for the selected package.

    Unless otherwise stated, standard transportation is included for services performed within Cancun, Riviera Maya, and Tulum.

    XVII.b) Additional coverage time, distant travel, separate venues, additional sessions, additional team members, or services outside the original package may result in additional charges.

    All additional services are subject to availability.

    XVII.c) Additional coverage requested on the event day is not guaranteed and must be approved by Jonhy & Rose.

    Any applicable additional-time rate will be communicated to the Client.

    XVIII. SPECIFIC PACKAGE TERMS & WRITTEN AGREEMENTS

    The Client’s selected package, invoice, written proposal, and any specifically negotiated inclusions form part of this Agreement.

    These may include, depending on the booking:

    • Total coverage hours
    • Number of photographers
    • Number of videographers
    • Welcome Party coverage
    • Rehearsal Dinner coverage
    • Additional sessions
    • Drone coverage
    • Film deliverables
    • Social media content
    • Additional hours
    • Travel or accommodation arrangements
    • Preferred Full Payment Rate
    • Promotional or complimentary services

    If a specific written term applicable to the Client’s booking differs from a general term in this Agreement, the specific written term will control only with respect to that particular item.

    All other provisions of this Agreement remain in effect.

    Complimentary services, bonuses, or promotional additions have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for refunds or credits unless expressly agreed in writing.

    XIX. REMEDIES & ENTIRE AGREEMENT

    Both parties agree to make reasonable efforts to fulfill their respective obligations under this Agreement.

    Material non-compliance may result in termination of the Agreement subject to the payment, cancellation, refund, and liability provisions stated herein.

    This Agreement, together with the Client’s selected package, invoice, proposal, and any written amendments accepted by both parties, represents the understanding between the Client and Jonhy & Rose regarding the contracted services.

    Changes to material terms should be confirmed in writing.

    By signing this Agreement, the Client acknowledges that they have reviewed and accepted the terms applicable to their selected service and payment method.

    XX. GOVERNING LAW

    This contract is governed by the laws of the State of Quintana Roo, Mexico.
