Your Wedding Day Guide

A few simple things to have ready, so the morning feels calm, nothing gets forgotten, and you can simply enjoy every moment while we take care of the rest.

i.

The getting ready rooms

Getting ready is where the story of your day begins. A calm, tidy space lets us create images that feel intimate and timeless.

  • Both rooms tidy and clear: bags, bottles and clothes put away.
  • Open curtains: natural light is our favorite.
  • Bridesmaids, groomsmen and family who will be in the photos arrive on time.
  • Each set of accessories waits in the room of the person who will wear it.

Choose your helper

We highly recommend asking a bridesmaid or family member to be our point person for the day: someone who knows the family, can gather people for portraits and help with any small detail. That way you can be completely relaxed.

ii.

For the bride

When we arrive, have your hair and makeup done and wear a robe or pajama set. Everything else stays aside so we can photograph each piece before you put it on.

  • Makeup and hair finished, robe or pajama on.
  • Your bridesmaids ready too: hair and makeup done, in their robes or pajamas.
  • Your dress ready on a wooden or special bridal hanger.
  • All accessories gathered in one place (see the details).
iii.

For the groom

When we arrive, be ready with your pants, white shirt and hair done. Keep every accessory aside, so we can capture the final touches as they happen.

  • Pants, white shirt and hair ready.
  • Jacket, tie and accessories kept aside in your room.

Your groomsmen

When we arrive, your groomsmen should be 100% dressed and ready, so the focus can be on you and those final touches.

iv.

The details

If styling photos are part of your collection, these are the pieces we love to photograph. Please have them together in each room before we arrive.

Bride’s room

  • Shoes
  • Veil
  • Necklace, bracelet and earrings
  • Rings
  • Vows
  • Perfume
  • Lipstick
  • Wedding invitation, with envelope
  • Any special gift or keepsake

Groom’s room

  • Shoes and socks
  • Belt
  • Watch
  • Tie or bow tie
  • Ring
  • Vows
  • Perfume
  • Any special gift or keepsake
v.

The first look

One of the most intimate moments of the day: the first time you see each other, just the two of you.

We recommend keeping it private. If the bride would like a hand with the dress or the bouquet, only the maid of honor joins, and then gives you your space.

  • Just the two of you (and the maid of honor, only if needed).
  • Phones away from the spot, so it stays yours.
  • We stay with you 20–30 minutes right after for couple portraits.

Why portraits before the ceremony

After the ceremony everything moves faster: cocktail hour, family, sunset, the reception. Taking your portraits right after the first look gives you a relaxed, unhurried moment together, and many more images of the two of you.

vi.

Your bridal party

We almost always recommend these photos right after the first look, while everyone is fresh and ready. In about 20 minutes we create:

  • Group photos with the full bridal party.
  • The bride with her bridesmaids.
  • The groom with his groomsmen.

This moment flows beautifully when everyone is dressed, nearby and ready when we call them. A little coordination from all makes it quick, fun and stress free.

vii.

A toast before the ceremony

Some of our favorite moments happen right here. If you would like a fun, joyful moment before the ceremony, have a bottle of champagne (or beers, shots, your special drink) and enough glasses for everyone, and share a toast with your bridesmaids, groomsmen and family.

“The best photos come from real moments, so give yourselves a few to simply celebrate.”

viii.

Family portraits

These portraits can happen before or after the ceremony. Organized, they take just a few minutes. Without a plan, they can feel long, and nobody wants to stand in the sun waiting.

Your family helper

Choose one person who knows both families well. They help us recognize everyone, gather each group and have the next one ready to step in.

Your shot list

Send us your list of family groups before the wedding, so we follow your order and nobody is missed.

  • Family stays close to the ceremony spot right after it ends.
  • As soon as one group is done, the helper calls the next one in.
  • Your helper builds each set with us, so every group is ready in seconds.

“In our experience, with a helper and a list, this part is effortless, and you get back to celebrating.”

ix.

Surprises & special moments

Planning a letter, a gift, a first look with your dad, a special dance or any surprise? Tell us in advance, even in a private email if it is a secret, so we are in the right place at the right moment.

Anything we don’t know about ahead of time is much harder to capture well.

x.

The day before

We will check in with you the day before the wedding. Having these answers ready makes our arrival smooth:

  • Room numbers for the bride and groom (or spa / special suite).
  • Our vendor access: day pass, vendor fee or hotel nights. If there is a room, under whose name.
  • Meals for the photographers on weddings of 5+ hours, ideally served at the same time as your guests so we can keep the reception timeline on track. We have no food restrictions.
A note on light

The golden hour

Sunset in the Caribbean comes fast. Your timeline is built around the most beautiful light of your day, so being on time with getting ready is what gives us those golden portraits.

For us, the most important thing is that you enjoy your wedding.
We’ll take care of the rest.
